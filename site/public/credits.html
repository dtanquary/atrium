Etching

  • Etching's pictures are traced, each as a single line, from old prints, plates, drawings and patents. From The Metropolitan Museum of Art's Open Access collection, CC0: Hokusai's Under the Wave off Kanagawa, Mishima Pass in Kai Province and Kajikazawa in Kai Province, Dürer's The Rhinoceros, a plate of Perspectiva Corporum Regularium by Jost Amman after Wenzel Jamnitzer, Palladio's Villa Rotonda, Leonardo da Vinci's A Bear Walking, al-Jazari's water clock of the peacocks and Valturio's siege engines. Leonardo's six machine drawings are from his Codex Madrid I: Imágenes procedentes de los fondos de la Biblioteca Nacional de España, CC BY 4.0. His aerial screw is from Charles Ravaisson-Mollien's facsimile of 1883 by way of the Internet Archive, and his wing from the Codex Atlanticus at the Veneranda Biblioteca Ambrosiana by way of Wikimedia Commons, both public domain. The flea and the ant are from Robert Hooke's Micrographia, from the Wellcome Collection (the flea, the ant), CC BY 4.0; the telescope, printing press, pump house, paddle steamer and bridge centres are Wellcome Collection prints in the public domain. Ramelli's fountain and Agricola's glass furnaces are from the Science History Institute, public domain. The balloons and Cayley's flying machine are from the Library of Congress, no known restrictions on publication. The ship draught is F. H. af Chapman's, from Sjöhistoriska museet, public domain. Tycho Brahe's observatory is from the Getty Research Institute, and the two Crystal Palace plates from McGill University Library, both by way of the Internet Archive, public domain. Three patterns are plates of Jules Bourgoin's Les éléments de l'art arabe, from the Getty Research Institute by way of the Internet Archive, public domain. The flying machine, the velocipede, the airship and the road engine are drawings from US patents 821,393 (O. and W. Wright), 59,915 (P. Lallement), 621,195 (F. Graf Zeppelin) and 549,160 (G. B. Selden), from the United States Patent and Trademark Office, public domain. The Pont Neuf is part of sheet 11 of Louis Bretez's Plan de Paris of 1739, engraved by Claude Lucas, from the Norman B. Leventhal Map & Education Center at the Boston Public Library (source), public domain. Hollar's Venus comb, nautilus and giant clams and the garden maze after Le Rouge are from the Rijksmuseum, public domain; Stubbs's horse skeleton and Hokusai's Fuji at Sea from The Met, CC0; the sea serpent and elephant after Gessner, the harbor crane and the steam plow are Wellcome Collection prints in the public domain. Eiffel's Garabit Viaduct is from the Universidad de Sevilla's copy, and the locomotive, Perronet's machine and van Zyl's sawmill from EPFL Library's, all by way of the Internet Archive, public domain. The lake house is Henry Lord Gay's, from the Art Institute of Chicago, CC0. The schooner Ernestina is a drawing of the Historic American Engineering Record, National Park Service (Caleb Reed, Matthew Jacobs, Katherine Whalen and Karolina Walichiewicz, 2009), from the Library of Congress, public domain. Holt's tractor and five of the aircraft are from US patent 874,008 and design patents D97,355 (Boeing), D143,822 and D92,654 (Lockheed), D128,027 (Douglas) and D129,529 (Consolidated), public domain; the Spitfire is NACA's drawing, from NASA, and the PBY Catalina is from its 1945 handbook, both public domain. The torpedo boat is Föreningen Sveriges Flotta's model drawing of 1946, from Sjöhistoriska museet, CC BY-SA 4.0. The dinosaurs are from O. C. Marsh's The Dinosaurs of North America (US Geological Survey, 1896), the British Museum (Natural History)'s guide of 1905 and C. W. Andrews's catalogue of 1910, H. F. Osborn's papers for the American Museum of Natural History (the T. rex skull, the T. rex, Struthiomimus) and W. D. Conybeare's sea dragons of 1824, public domain. Crater Lake (US Geological Survey), Iceland (Þorvaldur Thoroddsen) and Yosemite Valley are from the Leventhal Map & Education Center, no known copyright restrictions; the Sognefjord is from Kartverket, the Norwegian Mapping Authority: Kart over Nordre Bergenhus Amt (sø), Martinius Thorsen Kingo and H. N. Bergh, 1874; and the Mississippi's old courses are Harold N. Fisk's for the Mississippi River Commission, 1944, from LSU's scan, public domain. (list)
